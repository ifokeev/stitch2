Let Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or another assistant plan your week, log workouts and read your progress. It signs in as you, and you can remove it at any time.

Add this address to your assistant as a remote MCP server, often called a custom connector.

  • Add the address to your assistant.
  • A gymgym page opens: sign in and pick the account to use.
  • Choose Allow. The assistant can now work with that account.

    • It uses only the account you picked when it signed in.
    • It works in your units and language; its changes show up in the app at once.
